What happens
  • Expired plans close
    Subscriptions past expiry on auto-close plans are closed first.
  • One draft invoice per period
    Lines, taxes and totals copied from the subscription. A period already billed is skipped.
  • Discounts apply themselves
    The best valid discount is applied and its usage counted.
  • Billing date advances
    By the plan interval, with correct month-end handling.
